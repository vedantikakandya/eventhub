### 💜 EventHub — Discover. Connect. Experience.

**EventHub is your space to discover events, connect with people, and make the most of every experience around you.**
From exciting campus activities and workshops to talks, meetups, and community events, EventHub brings everything together in one simple place. No more missing out because you heard about an event too late or couldn't find the details you needed.

### ✨ Discover What's Happening
Explore events based on what interests you. Find event details, categories, locations, dates, organisers, and availability — all in one place.

### 🎟️ Find Your Next Experience
Whether you're looking for something fun, something useful, or simply something new, EventHub makes it easier to discover experiences worth showing up for.

### 🤝 Connect With Organisers
Have questions about an event? Get in touch with organisers directly and stay connected before and after the event.

### 💬 Stay Connected
EventHub makes communication easier with in-app messaging, helping attendees and organisers stay connected without jumping between different platforms.

### 🏫 Built With Communities in Mind
EventHub is designed especially with college and local communities in mind — places where there is always something happening, but it's surprisingly easy to miss it.

### 🚀 Organise & Grow
Event organisers can create and manage their events, while vendors can showcase their businesses and connect with potential customers.

**EventHub is more than an event listing app. It's about finding something worth attending, meeting people along the way, and turning ordinary days into memorable ones.**
